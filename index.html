Frequently Asked Questions

The essentials before you reserve.

Does DNA collection hurt?

No. Relationship DNA testing generally uses a simple cheek swab collected from inside the mouth.

How long does the appointment take?

Your visit includes check-in, paperwork, payment and specimen collection. Final appointment timing will be listed in your confirmation.

Who needs to attend?

That depends on the relationship test selected. Contact Synoplus before booking if you are unsure who should participate.

When will I receive my results?

Standard results typically arrive 3–5 business days after laboratory receipt, depending on the selected service.

Can I use my results in court?

If results may be used for a legal matter, select a legal chain-of-custody collection. Requirements can vary by proceeding.

Is my deposit refundable?

The reservation deposit is nonrefundable, subject to the disclosed reservation terms presented before payment.

Can I test without the alleged father?

Other relationship tests may be possible depending on which relatives are available. Contact us so we can help identify the appropriate test.

What if I don't know which test I need?

Contact Synoplus before reserving and we'll help you understand which relationship test fits your situation.
