Frequently Asked Questions

Does DNA collection hurt?

No. Collection uses a simple cheek swab.

How long does the appointment take?

Appointments are scheduled in 15-minute time slots.

Who needs to attend the appointment?

The required participants for the test selected should attend. Minor participants require their legal guardian.

When will I receive my results?

Standard results typically arrive 3–5 business days after laboratory receipt.

Can I use my results in court?

Choose Legal / Chain-of-Custody testing if the results are intended for legal use.

Is my deposit refundable?

The $50 reservation deposit is nonrefundable and is applied toward your testing total.

Can I test without the alleged father?

Other relationship testing options may help. Use Help Me Choose or call us to discuss the available participants.

Can children be tested?

Yes. If anyone tested is under 18, the legal guardian must be present to consent and the minor's birth certificate must be brought to the appointment.

What identification should I bring?

Bring government-issued identification and the additional documents listed in your appointment confirmation. For minors, a legal guardian must attend and bring the minor’s birth certificate.

What if I don't know which test I need?

Call us at 321-800-1676 and we'll help you choose.
